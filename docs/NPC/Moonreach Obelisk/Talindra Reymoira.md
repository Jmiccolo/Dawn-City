title: Talindra Reymoira
---

![[Talindra Reymoira.png]]

Talindra Reymoira leads the Druidic Circle of [[Ronaele Sanctuary]] in Dawn City. She was seated on the [[Triumvirate Arcanum Cognoscenti]] 30 years ago and has been a sage to all those seeking the beauty and healing that nature provides in the city.
