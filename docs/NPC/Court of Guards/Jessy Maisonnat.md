A Lieutenant in the Court of Guards, Jessy Maisonnat works with [[Sergeant Agrathar Colgane]] as a lead investigator into crimes committed within Dawn City. When the party was taken to the Court of Guards for questioning about the whereabouts of [[James Hawke]] they were the officer questioning the party.
