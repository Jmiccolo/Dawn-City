A water genasi found by [[Talindra Reymoira]] as a baby on the shores of Dawn City and cared for by [[Elder Craigory Eilvyre]], a leader in the druidic Circle that calls Dawn City their home, and [[Selphie Eilvyre]] of Ronaele's Sanctuary. Fern seems out of place amongst many in the city. Her blue skin and affinity for water caught the eye of [[James Hawke]] one day while visiting Fern's father. James brought Fern to [[The Docks]] to help him in an investigation where she found a box marked with three red claws as well as a large monster in the waters. James had been searching for the origins of Fern and her family.
