---
title: Fern
---

A water genasi named Fern was discovered as a baby on the shores of Dawn City by [[Talindra Reymoira]]. She was raised by [[Elder Craigory Eilvyre]], a prominent druid in the Circle that calls Dawn City home, and [[Selphie Eilvyre]] of Ronaele’s Sanctuary. Despite her upbringing in the city, Fern felt somewhat out of place among its inhabitants. Her blue skin and affinity for water drew the attention of [[James Hawke]] one day while he visited Fern’s father. Intrigued by her unique qualities, James brought Fern to [[The Docks]] to assist him in an investigation. During their visit, Fern stumbled upon a box marked with three red claws and encountered a large monster lurking in the waters. James had been searching for the origins of Fern and her family, and Fern’s discovery provided crucial clues in his quest.
