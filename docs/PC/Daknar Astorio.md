Daknar "Dak" Astorio, son of [[Gaggar Astorio]] who was the son of the former [[Obsidian Command]] General [[Nalkar Astorio]] works on the wall as a young soldier in the protection of Dawn City from that which might attack it from beyond its gates. While he feels the duty of his lineage to stick to the wall, he finds meaning in seeking Justice outside of the command, taking bounties wherever he can find them. He met [[James Hawke]] one fateful day on the wall when 3 dire wolves were chasing James and 2 others out of the forests of the [[ShadeMounts]] and to the wall. After disobeying his command to intercept the group, the direwolves were handled by James and [[Lucy Daring]] as [[Delilah Ornery]] was taken into the city to be protected.  Delilah feared for her brother's safety who she exclaimed was taken while traversing the ShadeMounts and James assured her they would find him. James took Daknar under his wing and showed him a thing or two about Bounty Hunting.
