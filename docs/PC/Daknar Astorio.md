# Daknar Astorio

Daknar “Dak” Astorio, the son of [[Gaggar Astorio]], the son of the former [[Obsidian Command]] General [[Nalkar Astorio]], works as a young soldier on the wall, protecting Dawn City from potential threats beyond its gates. While he feels compelled to follow in his lineage’s footsteps, he finds purpose in seeking justice outside the command, taking bounties whenever he can.

One fateful day, Dak encountered [[James Hawke]] on the wall. Three dire wolves were chasing James and two others out of the forests of the ShadeMounts and towards the wall. Despite his command to intercept the group, Dak disobeyed and watched as James and Lucy Daring handled the direwolves. [[Delilah Ornery]], one of the victims, was taken into the city for protection.

Delilah, fearing for her brother’s safety, exclaimed that he had been taken while traversing the ShadeMounts. James reassured her that they would find him. Taking Daknar under his wing, James taught him the ropes of bounty hunting.
