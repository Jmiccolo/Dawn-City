---
title: Argalyn
---

Argalyn, a dark-haired half-elf, strolls confidently through Merchant Alley, her eyes gleaming with anticipation. She believes she’ll find new items to sell or charming individuals to hire for her next endeavor. As a child of affluent parents, she’s been residing in Merchant Alley, but recently, she was informed that she must fend for herself.

[[Remy Beaujolais]], the owner of [[Beaujolais Commodities]], operates a small tent in the thoroughfare of Merchant Alley. He hired Argalyn as a secondhand to procure and hawk the goods he sells, offering her a meager salary in return. Partnering with [[Beaumont]], Argalyn’s confidence in her place in Merchant Alley grew steadily.

However, her good luck was shattered when an unknown man ran up and abruptly grabbed a satchel containing freshly obtained items by [[Beaumont]] from the storefront. This man turned out to be [[James Hawke]], and Argalyn was summoned for questioning about the origin of the goods and her relationship with him. Unaware that she had joined the party as an outsider, having never met James before, she found herself inexplicably tied to him.
