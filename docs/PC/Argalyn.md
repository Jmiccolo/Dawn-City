Argalyn, a dark haired half elf walks the Merchant Alley confident that she will find some new item to sell or new person to charm into hiring her for the next better thing. A child of affluent parents, she has been housed in Merchant Alley but recently was told she would have to make it on her own. [[Remy Beaujolais]], owner of [[Beaujolais Commodities]] runs a small tent in Merchant Alley's thoroughfare and hired Argalyn as a second hand to procure and hawk the goods he sold, giving her a pittance of a salary in return. Partnered with [[Beaumont]],  Argalyn was getting pretty confident in her place in Merchant Alley before one day a man ran up and grabbed a satchel containing items freshly obtain by [[Beaumont]] that he took off the back of a truck. This man turned out to be [[James Hawke]] and Argalyn was brought in for questioning about where the goods came from and who he was to her. Not having any idea she joined the party as an outsider, not having previously met James but inextricably now tied to him.
