Torbenik "Torque" Strongspark runs Strongspark Repairs, a local repair shop for appliances and some Arcanotech. His parents left him the shop to retire. He has many regular customers and helps his neighbor [[Alfokhuna Flintforged]] "Alfie" with her racing car every so often. His shop is his home and he takes good care of it. Seeing a man with a broken arcRider one day, Torque chose to help one [[James Hawke]] with getting the bike riding again and soon built a friendship around inventions and blueprints James would bring Torque to help him understand or build.
