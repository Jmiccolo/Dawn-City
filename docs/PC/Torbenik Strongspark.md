---
title: Torbenik Strongspark
---

Torbenik “Torque” Strongspark, the owner of [[Strongspark Repairs]], a local appliance and Arcanotech repair shop, inherited the business from his parents when they retired. He has a loyal customer base and occasionally assists his neighbor, [[Alfokhuna Flintforged]], “Alfie,” with her racing car. Torque considers his shop his home and takes great pride in maintaining it. One day, he encountered a man with a broken arcRider and decided to help him regain his riding skills. Through this experience, Torque and [[James Hawke]] formed a friendship based on their shared interest in inventions and blueprints.
