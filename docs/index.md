# Welcome to Dawn City
**Dawn City, a bustling metropolis of tall skyscrapers and taller mage towers, lights up and glimmers across the puddles of rain as millions of inhabitants traverse its clogged arteries of car-lined streets and train lines. The Triumvirate of the Arcanus Cognoscienti who built this city gave birth to new Arcanotech the world had never known, creating a seat of authority and privilege for those who could control it. The exportation of this Arcanotech drove trade and development throughout the city and brought people from all over to attempt their hand at extracting a piece of the riches for themselves. "If you can make it to Dawn City," a phrase often uttered by the optimistic in search of opportunity, is hardened by the fact that to travel to Dawn City can be dangerous. Those unable to pay their way onto airlines or ships into the city must brave the ShadeMounts, a desolate mountain range containing some of the most threatening magical beasts discovered. As the city grew in prominence, so has its underbelly. Organized crime, smuggling, money laundering, and all other seedy business has given rise to a secondary meaning of the phrase, "If you can make it to dawn” city. Those wishing to survive this city now do so through ignorance and hard work or by rising through the ranks of its darker side. How will you fair when the weight of the city bears down on you?**

---
### Player Characters

**As a player in Dawn City you take on a character whose life has been consumed by the city. Whether that is for the dreams of what it can provide or the weight that it puts on you. Your characters should take a part in the city and have a regular life outside of what might happen in the storyline.**

**That being said, sometimes the weight of the city becomes too much and a character might meet an end unforeseen. I am recommending a back up character at all times that will level with the main storyline.**

### Classes

**All DnD 5e published classes are up for grabs. Each class does have specific professions in the city as detailed in some organizations or locations. This does not mean your character cannot subvert expectations.**

### Races

**Dawn City is full of all different races and all published races are acceptable.**

### Backgrounds

**Custom backgrounds will be created for each character based on their backstory in the city. If you wish to stick to a specific background from the published DnD material ask the DM.**

## Gritty Realism

**The campaign will use the gritty realism rules from the dungeon master guide when it comes to short and long rests.**

**Short Rest: 1 per day, typically from a good night’s sleep.**

**Long Rest: Consistent rest over a weeks(7 days) time.**

**To track this, each session outside of combat will be a week’s time. There should be downtime where you are going about your PC’s daily life and the rest of the party can be involved or not. The hope is that the party becomes more entwined in the lives of others in the party as the campaign goes on.**

**The use of this system is simply to recognize the passage of time in the city. It might take far longer for specific plot points to play out in game than in a typical campaign where combat is almost daily. Smaller combats can be expected during the week but only One Large combat should be expected while there is downtime (except for when thresholds are met).**

## Weight of the City

**Guilds: The city is full of social groups, unions, companies, etc. that can constitute as guilds working against the interests of the party. These guilds will sometimes not agree with the actions of a PC.**

**Thresholds: Each guild holds a specific threshold of affront it may take before acting. These thresholds will be held behind the DM screen and be marked against individuals. The potential that an individual or the entire group breaking a threshold may be possible.**

**High Leveled 1v1: When an individual has crossed this threshold, the DM will hold a secret 1v1 session with the player to resolve. Players should prepare for this eventuality in case a threshold is broken. In the result of a death of a PC in this meeting, their death will be used to continue the storyline and should not be shared with the group until after the campaign has finished or their death has been fully realized by the party. Players in this situation should also refrain from using this player knowledge with their new character to persuade or inform other PCs of the information they are not aware of.**
