# Welcome To Dawn City

**Dawn City, a vibrant metropolis adorned with towering skyscrapers and even taller mage towers, comes alive with a dazzling display of lights as millions of inhabitants navigate its congested streets and train lines. The Triumvirate of the Arcanus Cognoscienti, the architects of this city, gave birth to groundbreaking Arcanotech, a technology unlike anything the world had ever witnessed. This innovation established Dawn City as a bastion of authority and privilege, accessible only to those who possessed the ability to wield its power. The exportation of this Arcanotech catalyzed trade and development throughout the city, attracting people from distant lands who sought to claim a share of its riches for themselves.**

  

**“If you can make it to Dawn City,” an optimistic phrase often uttered by those seeking opportunity, takes on a darker hue when one considers the perilous journey to reach this city. Those unable to afford the luxury of air travel or maritime transportation must brave the treacherous ShadeMounts, a desolate mountain range harboring some of the most formidable magical beasts known to existence. As the city’s prominence grew, so did its underbelly, giving rise to a sinister interpretation of the phrase. Organized crime, smuggling, money laundering, and other illicit activities flourished, transforming “If you can make it to Dawn” City into a warning of the dangers that await those who venture into its shadows.**

  

**Survival in this city now demands a combination of ignorance and perseverance, or the cunning ability to navigate its darker underworld. The weight of the city’s oppressive presence looms large, casting a shadow over the lives of its inhabitants. How will you fare when the city’s demands become overwhelming?**

  

---

### Player Characters

  

**As a player in Dawn City, you embody a character whose life has been intertwined with the city. Whether it’s driven by the allure of its potential or the burden it imposes, your character should actively participate in the city’s affairs and maintain a regular life beyond the unfolding storyline.**

  

**However, it’s important to acknowledge that the weight of the city can sometimes become overwhelming, leading to unexpected character deaths. To mitigate this risk, I strongly recommend creating a backup character who can seamlessly transition into the main storyline at the current level.**

  

### Classes

  

**All DnD 5e published classes are up for grabs. Each class does have specific professions in the city as detailed in some organizations or locations. This does not mean your character cannot subvert expectations.**

  

### Races

  

**Dawn City is full of all different races and all published races are acceptable.**

  

### Backgrounds

  

**Custom backgrounds will be created for each character based on their backstory in the city. If you wish to stick to a specific background from the published DnD material ask the DM.**

## Gritty Realism

  

**The campaign will use the gritty realism rules from the dungeon master guide when it comes to short and long rests.**

  

**Short Rest: 1 per day, typically from a good night’s sleep.**

  

**Long Rest: Consistent rest over a weeks(7 days) time.**

  

**To track this, each session outside of combat will span a week. During these downtime periods, you should engage in your PC’s daily activities, and the rest of the party can either participate or not. The goal is to foster a deeper connection between the party members and the lives of others in the party as the campaign progresses.**

  

**The use of this system is simply to recognize the passage of time in the city. It might take far longer for specific plot points to play out in game than in a typical campaign where combat is almost daily. Smaller combats can be expected during the week but only One Large combat should be expected while there is downtime (except for when thresholds are met).**

  

## Weight of the City

  

**Guilds: The city is full of social groups, unions, companies, etc. that can constitute as guilds working against the interests of the party. These guilds will sometimes not agree with the actions of a PC.**

  

**Thresholds: Each guild holds a specific threshold of affront it may take before acting. These thresholds will be held behind the DM screen and be marked against individuals. The potential that an individual or the entire group breaking a threshold may be possible.**

  

**High Leveled 1v1:** When a player crosses a threshold, the Dungeon Master (DM) will secretly hold a one-on-one session with the player to resolve the situation. Players should prepare for this eventuality in case a threshold is broken. If a player dies in this meeting, their death will be used to continue the storyline and should not be shared with the group until after the campaign ends or the party has fully realized the information. Players in this situation should also avoid using this player’s knowledge with their new character to persuade or inform other players of information they are unaware of.**
