title: The Leaky Pitcher
---

**The bar in [[Ashdenn]] that [[Lucy Daring]] brings the party to after being interrogated by [[Jessy Maisonnat]].** 

**Argalyn reveals her parents have left and she has been staying in the city on her own making ends meet. She has a supplier named [[Remy Beaujolais]].** 

**[[Lucy Daring]] talks about her connection to [[James Hawke]] in her search for her lost sister. She said he would be meeting her here but it has taken a while for him to get there.**
