The party comes across the office of [[James Hawke]] after a night at a bar with [[Lucy Daring]]. A storm has rolled in and rain begins to fall. The office building is a 4 story brownstone like building with a set of slightly worn marble stairs to the front door. James' office is the last door on the right and has a wooden door with a frosted glass window. The title "James Hawke Private Investigator" in gold lettering. The door is ajar opening to a secretary office with a half wall frosted window divider. The waiting room looks disheveled, an armchair is tipped over and acid has burned the edge of it. The brown leather couch has a large sword slash with burning at the tears through it. All of the items on the secretary, [[Carissa Cross]]', desk have been strewn across the floor: pencils, a stapler, the Appointment Book.  

Crossing the threshold into James's office we see a large office with a desk multiple shelves with books and filing cabinets. At one point a very organized chaos Dak now sees an office in disarray. Papers have exploded across the floor from open cabinet drawers, One bookshelf has been dropped to the floor. Blood splatters from blade strikes litter the many pictures showing James shaking hands with city leaders and victims he has saved or provided retribution for. 

A single line of burning energy has cut through the floor, the hardwood desk in the center of the room splitting it in half and up the wall. Tendrils of burned wood form the markings of electricity. With a proper investigation the party can tell that this line extends about 30 feet to the alley wall in the next building.

A map on the wall shows that multiple explosions similar to what was experienced at the last known sight of [[Talindra Reymoira]] have been found around the city. Still no known suspect but the bomb is definitely of Arcanotech making.

A corner of the office wall is plastered with posters of upcoming fights at [[Rumpus Park]]. They will see the name The Plundered Purse as well as a party of 4 members of what looks like a barbarian, rogue, monk and fighter. Pictures of Owlbears, Basilisk, and a Bluespawn Stormlizard are on the docket for the monster fights.  

A box of blueprints sits next to one of the filing cabinets. In inspecting a blueprint, the party will see that these blueprints are from the [[The Lusterworks]].

A bag of products from Argalyn's job is sitting in a corner. Upon investigation you will find 3 sets of bracers, an empty box with 3 claw marks in a red circle, odds and ends and a spherical device.
