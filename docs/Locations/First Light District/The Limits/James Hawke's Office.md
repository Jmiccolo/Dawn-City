**The party, having had a night out with [[Lucy Daring]], stumbles upon [[James Hawke]]’s office after a storm rolls in and rain pours down. The four-story brownstone building has slightly worn marble stairs leading to the front door. James’s office, the last one on the right, has a wooden door adorned with frosted glass and the title “James Hawke Private Investigator” in gold lettering. The door is ajar, revealing a secretary’s office with a half-wall frosted window divider. The waiting room appears disheveled, with an armchair tipped over and acid burns on its edge. A brown leather couch bears a large sword slash and burns through the tears. All the items on Carissa Cross’s desk are strewn across the floor: pencils, a stapler, and the Appointment Book.**

**Crossing the threshold into James’s office, the party is met with a chaotic scene. Papers have exploded across the floor from open cabinet drawers, and one bookshelf has fallen to the ground. Blood splatters from blade strikes litter the numerous pictures showing James shaking hands with city leaders and victims he has saved or provided retribution for.**

**A single line of burning energy has cut through the floor, splitting the hardwood desk in half and up the wall. Tendrils of burned wood form the markings of electricity. Through a thorough investigation, the party can determine that this line extends approximately 30 feet to the alley wall in the next building.**

**A map on the wall reveals that multiple explosions similar to the one experienced at the last known sighting of Talindra Reymoira have been found around the city. Despite the lack of a known suspect, the bomb is undoubtedly of Arcanotech origin.**

**A corner of the office wall is adorned with posters of upcoming fights at Rumpus Park. The posters feature the name “The Plundered Purse” and a group of four individuals who appear to be a barbarian, a rogue, a monk, and a fighter. Additionally, pictures of Owlbears, Basilisks, and a Bluespawn Stormlizard are listed for the monster fights.**

**Next to a filing cabinet, a box of blueprints from The Lusterworks is found. Upon examining these blueprints, the party will gain valuable insights into the company’s ongoing projects.**

**In a corner, a bag containing various items from Argalyn’s job is discovered. Among the contents are three sets of bracers, an empty box marked with three claw marks in a red circle, miscellaneous items, and a spherical device.**
