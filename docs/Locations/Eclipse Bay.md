---
title: Eclipse Bay
---

Tucked away on the northern part of the [[Soul Arbiter Coast]], Eclipse Bay welcomes those willing to risk their lives at the thrashing seas to the visage of Dawn City. Entering through the [[Straight of the Crater]] is the only entrance to Eclipse Bay. Small boats and sailing vessels must take heed when traversing this dangerous passage as the obsidian rock formations can cut the hull and leave travellers bashed against its cliffs. Large shipping vessels have found their ways through good navigation and ironsides in order to make it to [[The Docks]]. The runoff of years of wastewater treatment has left the Bay murky, hiding the dangers it might contain.
